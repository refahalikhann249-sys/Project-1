Healthy Food

Fill your plate with real food. Colour means nutrients.

🥗

Fruits & Vegetables

Aim for five portions a day. They supply fibre, vitamins and potassium.

🌾

Whole Grains

Ragi, brown rice, oats and millets keep you full for longer.

🥜

Protein

Dals, eggs, curd, nuts and sprouts help muscles repair and grow.

🍬

Cut Back

Limit sugary drinks, deep-fried snacks and packaged foods.

Exercise & Fitness

You do not need a gym. Start with 30 minutes a day.

🚶

Walking

Brisk walking for 30 minutes protects the heart and lifts mood.

🧘

Yoga & Stretching

Improves flexibility, posture and reduces stress.

🏋️

Strength

Squats, push-ups and planks twice a week build strong bones.

🚴

Cardio

Cycling, skipping or swimming raises stamina.

Sleep & Hydration

😴 Better Sleep

  • Sleep and wake at the same time daily
  • Keep screens away 30 minutes before bed
  • Keep the room dark, cool and quiet
  • Avoid caffeine after 4 PM

💧 Stay Hydrated

  • Start the day with a glass of water
  • Carry a bottle with you
  • Drink more in hot weather and after exercise
  • Pale yellow urine means you are well hydrated
